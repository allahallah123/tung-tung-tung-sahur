Special Containment Procedures

SCP-173 is to be kept in a locked container at all times. Personnel entering the chamber must ensure direct line-of-sight is maintained and should verbally confirm blinking before any eye closure.

Description

SCP-173 appears as a concrete statue with traces of rebar and spray paint. The entity remains motionless while observed, but can move at high speed when line-of-sight is interrupted.

  • Do not turn your back.
  • Do not blink without warning your team.
  • Never leave the chamber unsecured.
